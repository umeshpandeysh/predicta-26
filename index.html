Module A: Multivariate Anomaly Detection & ML Inference Workstation

Isolate spatial outliers and evaluate physical semiconductor telemetry against operational risk thresholds.

Quick Summary — Module A Anomaly Screening

Module A identifies components showing abnormal current leakage or delay metrics compared to their lot population baseline.

Engineering Details & Math Models
Statistical Models Applied:
1. Robust PAT Z-Score (Median/MAD): $Z = \frac{x - \text{median}}{1.4826 \cdot \text{MAD}}$ (Z > 3.0 flagged)
2. COPOD Empirical Copula Density: 16-parameter multivariate tail probability estimation (Score > 2.5 flagged)
3. Frozen Production XGBoost V1: Operating Threshold $T=0.20$
