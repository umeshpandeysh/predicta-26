How PREDICTA Works

PHASE 1 01

1. Measure

Capture physical telemetry measurements ($I_{ddq}$ standby current, gate leakage, $t_{pd}$ delay, $V_{dd}$, temperature) at post-burn-in stress hours.

PHASE 2 02

2. Detect

Run PAT robust median Z-scoring and COPOD multivariate density estimation (Module A) to isolate spatial outliers and infant mortality units.

PHASE 3 03

3. Predict

Model physics-informed BTI degradation kinetics ($t^{0.2}$ power law) via GPR (Module B) to forecast drift up to 168 burn-in hours.

PHASE 4 04

4. Decide

Execute XGBoost inference ($T=0.45$) to route components into PASS ($P < 0.35$), MONITOR ($0.35 \le P < 0.65$), or REJECT ($P \ge 0.65$).

Engineering Details & Math Models

1. Robust PAT Z-Score (Median/MAD): $Z = \frac{x - \text{median}}{1.4826 \cdot \text{MAD}}$ (Z > 3.0 flagged)

3. Frozen Production XGBoost V1: Operating Threshold $T=0.45$
