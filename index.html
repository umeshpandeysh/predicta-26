3-Zone Operational Decision Engine

Risk classification matrix, 3-zone operational decision boundaries, and operator triage workflow.

ZONE 1: PASS
P < 0.20

ZONE 2: MONITOR
0.20 ≤ P < 0.65
Secondary Test Required (Elevated Model Risk P ≥ 0.20 or Reliability Evidence)
ZONE 3: REJECT
P ≥ 0.65
Critical Fail (High Model Risk P ≥ 0.65 or Critical Reliability Evidence)
