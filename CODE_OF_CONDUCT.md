# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of PREDICTA pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

* Use welcoming, inclusive, and professional technical language.
* Be respectful of differing viewpoints, design approaches, and engineering feedback.
* Gracefully accept constructive technical criticism.
* Focus on safety, reliability, scientific integrity, and maintainability.
* Show empathy towards all engineering team members and contributors.
