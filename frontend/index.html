Semiconductor Predictive Screening Workstation

PREDICTA analyzes post-burn-in physical telemetry measurements to detect early-life semiconductor degradation, anomalous current leakage, and drift kinetics prior to field deployment.

3-Step Predictive Screening Workflow

Step 1

Telemetry & Quality Gate

Validates raw physical parameters (voltage, current leakage, temperature, propagation delay) against physical boundaries prior to ML evaluation.

Step 2

Dual-Module Physics Screening

Module A detects multivariate statistical anomalies; Module B models kinetic BTI drift degradation curves over time (t0.2).

Step 3

3-Zone Operational Triage

XGBoost classifier (Operating Threshold: 0.45) routes components into PASS, SECONDARY TEST (borderline re-screen), or CRITICAL FAIL.
