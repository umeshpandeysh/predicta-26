# Phase 3: Dataset & Component Research

Details the physical parameters and reliability research forming the evidence foundation of Predicta.

## 1. Component & Datasheet Catalog
*   **SN74LVC1G04 (TI):** Quiescent current ($I_{ddq}$) and cell delay ($t_{pd}$) limits.
*   **IRF540N (Infineon):** Power MOSFET leakage current limits.
*   **UT54ACS04 (Cobham):** Space-grade rad-hard inverter specifications.

## 2. Physics-of-Failure Mechanics
*   **Bias Temperature Instability (BTI):** Traps charge carriers in the oxide layers, shifting threshold voltage ($V_{th}$) and delaying cell propagation timings ($t_{pd}$).
*   **Time-Dependent Dielectric Breakdown (TDDB):** Causes oxide deterioration, spiking gate leakage ($I_{leak}$) and standby currents ($I_{ddq}$).
